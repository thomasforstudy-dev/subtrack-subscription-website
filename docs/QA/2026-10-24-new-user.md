## Confusing for a new user

- “Decimal”, “null”, “Extractor: rules v1” and evidence/revision terminology are
  prominent in ordinary bill entry. Plain explanations would reduce the learning
  needed before saving a first receipt.
- Bills still say automatic OCR arrives in the “next slice”, while an actual
  **Read it for me** action and draft workflow already exist. Queued jobs depend
  on a separately run reader; “next time the reader runs” gives no estimate or
  owner setup instruction.
- Without a key, forecast says Gemini is “busy or out of free quota”; that is
  polite but obscures the missing-configuration cause. The reader can say it is
  using browser voice while its selector still names Gemini.
- The dashboard's renewal count spans currencies, while the adjacent amount is
  only the main currency. It is numerically consistent, but a newcomer may read
  it as the cost of every plan counted.
- Subscription export is page-scoped, while bill export is account-scoped.
  The explanatory text is present, but the difference is easy to miss.
- Unknown Subby questions invite contacting the owner even when no contact
  channel is configured. The fallback states that limitation honestly; it still
  leaves a new user without a useful next step.
